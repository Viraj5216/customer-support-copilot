1. REFUND POLICY
==================================================

1.1 Standard Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of receiving a physical product.

To qualify for a standard refund:

- The product must be returned within the applicable refund window.
- The product must be in its original condition.
- The product must include all major accessories supplied with the original order.
- The customer must provide the original order number.
- The product must not show signs of intentional damage or misuse.

Refund eligibility is determined based on the date the customer received the product, not the date the order was placed.

1.2 Products Not Normally Eligible for Refunds

The following products are normally not eligible for a standard refund:

- Digital products after download or activation.
- Gift cards after purchase.
- Products that have been intentionally damaged.
- Products with significant signs of misuse.
- Items specifically marked as "final sale."

Exceptions may be considered by a support manager when the customer reports a manufacturing defect or fulfillment error.

1.3 Damaged Products

Customers who receive a damaged product should contact support within 7 calendar days of delivery.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs showing:

- The product damage.
- The packaging.
- The shipping label.

If the damage is confirmed to have occurred before or during delivery, the customer may be offered:

- A replacement.
- A full refund.
- A partial refund where appropriate.

A damaged-product case reported after 7 days requires manual review.

1.4 Wrong or Missing Items

If a customer receives an incorrect product, they should contact support within 14 calendar days of delivery.

If an item is missing from an order, the customer should report the missing item within 14 calendar days.

Confirmed fulfillment errors are eligible for replacement or refund.

1.5 Sale and Discounted Products

Products purchased during a promotional sale are eligible for refunds unless the product was explicitly marked as final sale.

The refund amount is based on the amount actually paid by the customer rather than the product's original list price.

1.6 Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be considered when:

- A product has a minor defect that does not prevent normal use.
- An accessory is missing but the customer chooses to keep the product.
- A manager approves compensation for a service failure.

The amount of a partial refund is determined by the support team.

1.7 Shipping Cost Refunds

Standard outbound shipping charges are normally non-refundable for voluntary returns.

Shipping charges may be refunded when:

- The wrong product was shipped.
- The product arrived damaged.
- The product has a confirmed manufacturing defect.
- The return was caused by an error made by the company.

1.8 Refund Processing

After the returned product has been received and inspected, approved refunds are normally initiated within 5–7 business days.

The actual time for funds to appear depends on the customer's bank or payment provider.

Support must not promise an exact date for receiving funds.

1.9 Refund Payment Method

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method.

Refunds cannot normally be redirected to another payment method without manager approval.

If the original payment method is unavailable, the case requires manual review.

1.10 Refund Requests After 30 Days

Requests submitted after the standard 30-day refund period are normally not eligible.

A support manager may approve an exception when there is a documented:

- Manufacturing defect.
- Fulfillment error.
- Delivery problem.
- Other exceptional circumstance.

The AI must not promise an exception before manager approval.

1.11 Duplicate Charges

If a customer reports being charged more than once for the same order, support must verify the transaction information before promising a refund.

Confirmed duplicate charges should be escalated to the billing workflow.

The AI must not claim that a duplicate charge has been confirmed unless transaction data has been verified.

1.12 Fraud and Abuse Indicators

The following situations require manual review:

- Multiple unusual refund requests from the same customer.
- Repeated claims of damaged products without supporting evidence.
- Suspicious payment activity.
- Attempts to obtain refunds outside policy through repeated claims.

The AI must not accuse a customer of fraud. It should route the case for human review.

1.13 Manager Approval

Manager approval is required for:

- Refunds outside the standard eligibility period.
- Exceptions to final-sale restrictions.
- Refunds to an alternative payment method.
- High-value exceptions.
- Cases involving suspected fraud or unusual refund patterns.

The AI may recommend escalation but must not represent manager approval as already having occurred.

2. RETURN POLICY
==================================================

2.1 Standard Returns

Customers may return eligible physical products within 30 calendar days of receiving the order.

Returned products should be:

- In their original condition.
- Packed securely.
- Accompanied by the original order information where available.

2.2 Return Shipping

For voluntary returns, the customer may be responsible for return shipping costs.

Return shipping may be covered by Acme Commerce when the return is caused by:

- A damaged product.
- A wrong product.
- A confirmed manufacturing defect.
- A fulfillment error.

2.3 Non-Returnable Products

The following are normally non-returnable:

- Digital products after activation.
- Gift cards.
- Items explicitly marked final sale.
- Products damaged through customer misuse.

2.4 Inspection

Returned products may be inspected before a refund is approved.

If inspection identifies significant damage or misuse, the refund may be denied or adjusted.

2.5 Return Exceptions

Returns outside the standard return period require manual review.

Support agents must not guarantee an exception before approval.


==================================================
3. SHIPPING POLICY
==================================================

3.1 Standard Shipping

Standard shipping normally takes 5–7 business days after dispatch.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may be affected by:

- Weather.
- Carrier delays.
- Public holidays.
- Customs processing.
- Other circumstances outside company control.

3.2 Express Shipping

Express shipping normally takes 1–2 business days after dispatch.

The estimate does not include order-processing time before dispatch.

3.3 Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days.

During major sales, holidays, or unusually high demand, processing may take longer.

Customers receive a shipping confirmation email once their order has been dispatched.

3.4 Order Tracking

Customers can track orders using the tracking link in the shipping confirmation email.

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

3.5 Delayed Orders

If an order has not arrived within 10 business days after dispatch, the customer should contact support.

Support should verify current carrier tracking information before promising a delivery date.

The AI must not invent a delivery date.

3.6 Lost Shipments

A shipment may be treated as potentially lost when:

- Tracking has not updated for an extended period.
- The carrier reports that the package may be lost.
- The expected delivery period has passed significantly.

Potentially lost shipments require verification before replacement or refund.

3.7 Damaged Shipments

Customers who receive damaged packages should contact support as soon as possible.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs of:

- The package.
- Shipping label.
- Damaged product.

Confirmed damage may qualify for replacement or refund under the Refund Policy.

3.8 Shipping Address Changes

Customers may request an address change before dispatch.

Once an order has been dispatched, the address normally cannot be changed.

Address changes after dispatch require manual review.

3.9 Delivered but Not Received

If tracking shows an order as delivered but the customer states they did not receive it, support must verify delivery information.

The AI must not automatically declare a shipment lost or stolen.

3.10 International Shipping

International deliveries may take longer because of customs processing, carrier delays, and local regulations.

Customers may be responsible for customs duties, taxes, or import charges unless otherwise stated at checkout.

3.11 Shipping Escalation

Shipping cases should normally be escalated when:

- Tracking is unavailable or contradictory.
- The shipment may be lost.
- Delivery appears to be to the wrong address.
- The order is significantly delayed.
- A refund or replacement requires an exception.
- The customer is highly distressed and the issue remains unresolved.


==================================================
4. CANCELLATION POLICY
==================================================

4.1 Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation before an order has been dispatched.

Cancellation requests after dispatch cannot normally be processed.

4.2 Cancellation After Payment

Payment does not guarantee that an order can still be cancelled.

If the order has already entered fulfillment, cancellation may no longer be possible.

4.3 Cancelled Orders

If cancellation is approved before dispatch, the customer is normally refunded to the original payment method.

4.4 Subscription Cancellation

Subscription cancellation rules are described in the Subscription Policy.

4.5 Cancellation Exceptions

Requests to cancel an order after dispatch require manual review.

Support must not promise cancellation before confirming the order status.


==================================================
5. DAMAGED PRODUCT POLICY
==================================================

5.1 Reporting Damage

Customers should report damaged products within 7 calendar days of delivery.

Customers may be required to provide photographs of:

- Product damage.
- Packaging.
- Shipping label.

5.2 Available Resolutions

Depending on the circumstances, Acme Commerce may offer:

- Replacement.
- Full refund.
- Partial refund.

5.3 Manufacturing Defects

A manufacturing defect may qualify for replacement or refund even when the standard return period has expired, subject to review.

5.4 Customer-Caused Damage

Damage caused by misuse, accidents, unauthorized modifications, or intentional actions is normally not covered.

5.5 Escalation

Cases involving disputed responsibility for damage should be escalated for human review.

6. WARRANTY POLICY
==================================================

6.1 Warranty Period

Eligible products include a standard limited warranty of 12 months from the original purchase date unless the product listing specifies a different warranty period.

6.2 Warranty Coverage

The warranty may cover manufacturing defects that occur during normal intended use.

6.3 Warranty Exclusions

The warranty normally does not cover:

- Accidental damage.
- Liquid damage.
- Unauthorized modifications.
- Normal cosmetic wear.
- Damage caused by misuse.
- Damage caused by improper installation.

6.4 Warranty Claims

Customers may be asked to provide:

- Order number.
- Product serial number.
- Description of the problem.
- Photos or diagnostic information when appropriate.

6.5 Warranty Resolution

Depending on the issue, the company may provide:

- Troubleshooting.
- Repair.
- Replacement.
- Other approved resolution.

6.6 Warranty Escalation

Warranty cases involving disputed eligibility or unusual circumstances require manual review.

7. PAYMENT AND BILLING POLICY
==================================================

7.1 Accepted Payment Methods

Acme Commerce accepts supported payment methods displayed at checkout.

Available methods may vary depending on location and product.

7.2 Failed Payments

If a payment fails, the order may not be confirmed.

Customers should verify their payment details or use another supported payment method.

The AI must not claim that an order was successfully paid unless transaction information confirms it.

7.3 Duplicate Charges

Customers reporting duplicate charges require transaction verification.

A refund should not be promised until the duplicate transaction has been confirmed.

7.4 Pending Transactions

A pending payment may represent an authorization rather than a completed charge.

Customers should not automatically be told that a pending authorization is a completed payment.

If the status remains unresolved, the case should be escalated.

7.5 Refunds

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method.

Alternative payment methods require manual approval.

7.6 Billing Disputes

Billing disputes involving unusual transactions, payment inconsistencies, or potentially fraudulent activity require human review.

Support must not accuse customers of fraud.


==================================================
8. ACCOUNT AND SECURITY POLICY
==================================================

8.1 Password Reset

Customers who forget their password should use the official password-reset process.

Support agents must never ask customers to provide their password.

8.2 Account Verification

Certain account actions require identity verification.

Support must follow the approved verification process before making sensitive account changes.

8.3 Account Lockout

Repeated failed login attempts may temporarily lock an account.

Customers should follow the account recovery process.

If recovery fails, the case should be escalated.

8.4 Personal Information

Support agents must not request unnecessary sensitive information.

Customers should not send passwords, authentication codes, or complete payment-card information through support messages.

8.5 Suspicious Account Activity

Reports of unauthorized access, compromised accounts, or suspicious transactions require priority handling and human review.

8.6 Security Escalation

The following should be escalated immediately:

- Suspected account compromise.
- Unauthorized transactions.
- Exposure of authentication credentials.
- Repeated failed recovery attempts.


==================================================
9. SUBSCRIPTION POLICY
==================================================

9.1 Subscription Plans

Subscription plans renew according to the billing interval selected by the customer.

The applicable price is displayed before subscription confirmation.

9.2 Automatic Renewal

Subscriptions normally renew automatically unless cancelled before the next renewal date.

9.3 Subscription Cancellation

Customers may cancel future renewals through the account subscription settings.

Cancellation normally prevents future renewal but does not automatically refund previously completed billing periods.

9.4 Subscription Refunds

Refund requests for completed subscription periods are evaluated according to the Refund Policy and may require manual review.

9.5 Free Trials

Free trials may automatically convert into paid subscriptions if the customer does not cancel before the trial ends, when clearly disclosed during signup.

9.6 Billing Problems

Unexpected subscription charges should be verified against subscription and payment records.

The AI must not promise a refund without sufficient information.

9.7 Subscription Escalation

Escalate cases involving:

- Repeated unexpected charges.
- Cancellation failures.
- Conflicting billing information.
- Requests outside standard policy.
- Suspected unauthorized account access.

10. ESCALATION AND SUPPORT POLICY
==================================================

10.1 Purpose

The AI support system is designed to assist human support teams. It must not automatically resolve cases when the available information is insufficient or when company policy requires human judgment.

10.2 Human Escalation Conditions

A ticket should normally be escalated when:

- The issue falls outside documented policy.
- The customer requests an exception.
- Manager approval is required.
- Transaction information must be verified.
- Security or account compromise is suspected.
- Fraud indicators are present.
- The retrieved policy information is insufficient.
- Policies appear to conflict.
- The customer is highly distressed.
- The case has already failed to resolve through previous support interactions.

10.3 Sentiment and Escalation

A highly distressed or severely frustrated customer should normally receive human review when the underlying issue remains unresolved.

Sentiment alone does not determine whether a refund or other business action is approved.

10.4 Priority Levels

LOW:
General questions and minor issues with no immediate business impact.

MEDIUM:
Standard issues that require assistance but are not time-sensitive.

HIGH:
Issues involving significant customer impact, repeated failures, serious delays, or urgent business consequences.

CRITICAL:
Security incidents, suspected account compromise, major payment issues, or situations requiring immediate human intervention.

10.5 AI Limitations

The AI must not:

- Invent company policies.
- Invent transaction information.
- Claim that a refund was approved when it was not.
- Claim that a manager approved an exception when no approval exists.
- Accuse a customer of fraud.
- Promise an exact delivery date without verified information.
- Request a customer's password or authentication code.

10.6 Insufficient Information

If the system cannot determine the correct resolution from the available ticket information and knowledge base, it should ask for the necessary information or escalate to a human.

10.7 Response Tone

Responses should be:

- Professional.
- Clear.
- Concise.
- Respectful.
- Appropriate to the customer's emotional state.

Highly frustrated customers should receive empathetic language without making unsupported promises.
